## Research Question

How should the three-way Zelda timeline split (Downfall, Child, Adult) be described in terms of narrative structure and unique themes, where do Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom most plausibly belong, and which characters are branch-specific versus cross-branch?

## Scope and Exclusions

In scope:
- Canon split produced from Ocarina of Time outcomes
- Branch descriptions with distinct thematic identity
- Placement hypotheses for Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom
- Character distribution by branch, including Tingle
- Standard report sequence (00-03) and a complete reader HTML with navigation sidebar

Out of scope:
- Exhaustive item-level chronology for every side game and remaster
- Non-canon crossover titles (except where clearly flagged)
- Definitive resolution of unresolved canon questions absent an official explicit statement

## Methodology

1. Establish baseline timeline model from official compendia (Hyrule Historia, Zelda Encyclopedia) and first-party game text.
2. Distinguish primary evidence (games, official books) from secondary synthesis (fan wiki).
3. Map each branch by core events, game clusters, and branch-unique themes.
4. Evaluate BOTW/TOTK placement using claim-evidence weighting:
   - explicit official statements,
   - internal world-state evidence,
   - continuity constraints from each branch.
5. Build a character matrix for branch-specific versus cross-branch presence.
