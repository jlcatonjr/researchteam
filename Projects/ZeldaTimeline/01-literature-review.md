## 1. Overview of the Field

Discussion of Zelda chronology is built on a layered evidence stack rather than a single complete canonical statement. The strongest sources are official timeline publications and in-game text. Community reconstructions are extensive and often useful, but they are secondary and should not outrank official records.

The most influential baseline is the post-Ocarina-of-Time three-branch model published in Hyrule Historia and subsequently updated in Zelda Encyclopedia (Nintendo 2011; Nintendo 2018). These branch labels are usually treated as:

- Downfall Timeline: the line where the Hero of Time is defeated.
- Child Timeline: the line where Link returns and warns Zelda.
- Adult Timeline: the line left behind after Link disappears from that era.

## 2. Core Canon Sources

Hyrule Historia (2011) provided the first widely circulated official branch structure and anchored chronology for many pre-BOTW titles (Nintendo 2011). Zelda Encyclopedia (2018) expanded lore and chronology framing, while also preserving some ambiguity and editorial caveats around exact sequencing details (Nintendo 2018).

Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom are primary evidentiary works for late-era placement because they provide direct world-state clues: historical sedimentation, ruin ecologies, surviving races, references to prior eras, and institutional memory within Hyrule (Nintendo EPD 2017; Nintendo EPD 2023).

Creating a Champion is especially important for BOTW-era framing, including era descriptions that suggest an extremely distant placement relative to earlier branch conflicts (Nintendo 2017).

## 3. Key Debates

### 3.1 Is BOTW/TOTK in one branch or post-convergence memory space?

One camp argues BOTW/TOTK belong to a single branch very far in the future; another argues the games are intentionally placed at a distance where older branch distinctions become mythic and partially collapsed in practical significance.

### 3.2 How literal should compendium timelines be treated?

Official books are authoritative for broad chronology, but Zelda has a long tradition of retroactive lore alignment. This has led many scholars of game canon to treat timeline edges as "hard center, soft margins": central branch logic is firm, finer ordering may be revised in later works.

### 3.3 Character continuity versus role continuity

Many Zelda figures are title/role recurrences rather than single continuous individuals. Therefore, branch comparison often distinguishes:

- Identity continuity: same individual,
- Archetype continuity: recurring title/lineage/function.

This distinction is crucial for Link, Zelda, Impa, and forms of Ganon/Ganondorf.

## 5. Synthesis and Research Gap

The literature agrees on the branch structure but remains divided on late-era placement mechanics for BOTW and TOTK. The key gap is not whether the split exists, but how much explanatory power each branch retains at extreme chronological distance. This report addresses that gap through branch-specific thematic analysis plus a constraint-based placement argument.
