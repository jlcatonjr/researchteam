## SiezureTransition Project Enhancement

---

## KEY FINDINGS (Ranked by Relevance)

### A. Case Reports / Real-World Use Documentation
**IDENTIFIED:** ZERO case reports of methylene blue for seizure treatment in humans (2018-2026)
- No published case reports in MEDLINE/PubMed
- No clinical practice case series
- No off-label use documentation
- No epilepsy patient presentations reported
- **Implication:** Methylene blue is NOT adopted for seizure treatment in any documented clinical capacity

**Confidence:** HIGH (systematic absence across all expected publication venues)

---

### B. New Safety Signals (Beyond Zuschlag 2018)
**IDENTIFIED:** ZERO new adverse event clusters in peer-reviewed literature (2019-2026)
- Serotonin syndrome (Zuschlag 2018: 50 cases) remains the dominant documented safety concern
- Hemolysis risk in G6PD deficiency (Youngster 2010) unchanged
- No novel toxicity patterns reported in literature
- **Implication:** Real-world safety profile is consistent with documented literature; no undocumented epidemics evident in peer-reviewed sources

**Caveat:** FAERS and poison control aggregate data NOT systematically reviewed in published literature—raw data may exist but is not aggregated/published

**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH (peer-reviewed literature assessment complete; direct database searches not performed)

---

### C. Clinical Practice Adoption
**IDENTIFIED:** ZERO evidence of routine clinical use for seizures
- No clinical practice surveys or prescribing data
- No guideline recommendations (ILAE, AAN, EAN)
- No neurologist endorsement or clinical education materials
- No professional society position statements
- **Implication:** Methylene blue is NOT part of standard epilepsy care

**Confidence:** HIGH

---

### D. Social/Off-Label Use Patterns
**IDENTIFIED:** Anecdotal online interest; NOT documented in peer-reviewed clinical literature
- Biohacking/nootropics communities (Reddit, online forums) discuss MB for neuroprotection/cognitive enhancement
- **These discussions occur outside peer-reviewed medicine**
- NO case reports of patients presenting to clinics with self-medicated methylene blue history for seizures
- NO physician-supervised off-label seizure use documentation
- **Implication:** Online interest in MB has NOT translated to clinical adoption or documented off-label seizure practice

**Confidence:** MEDIUM (online communities observed; peer-reviewed absence confirmed)

---

### E. Adverse Events: Documented vs. Real-World
**IDENTIFIED:** 50+ documented serotonin syndrome cases (Zuschlag 2018) + hemolysis risk; additional real-world events UNKNOWN

**What We Know (Peer-Reviewed):**
- 50 documented serotonin syndrome cases
- Mechanism established (MAO inhibition)
- Risk modifiers identified (polypharmacy, dose, age)
- G6PD hemolysis risk documented

**What We Don't Know (Requires Direct Data Query):**
- Total FAERS adverse event reports (FDA database)
- Poison control center case volume (2018-2026)
- International pharmacovigilance aggregate data (WHO, EMA)
- Whether real-world AE counts exceed documented literature

**Recommendation:** FAERS and poison control data queries would be needed to fully answer whether MORE adverse events exist than captured in literature

**Confidence:** MEDIUM (literature assessment complete; direct administrative databases not queried)
