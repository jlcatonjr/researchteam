## Bibliography explorer (#210)

Operator request, relayed by the OrthodoxLLM session: a bibliography explorer whose reusable part is developed
and stored in researchteam, with OrthodoxLLM integrating it into its figures application. Navigation: period,
author, brief bio with attributes and the sources behind them, the author's works, and source detail with a
complete citation and summary.

- **Division of work.** researchteam holds the data contract, the exporter, the display component, an invented
  example and tests. OrthodoxLLM maps its files into the input tables, writes bios and summaries, and integrates
  the component. No OrthodoxLLM data came into this repository; the inventory was exchanged as shapes only.
- **Delivery.** Vendor-copy at a pinned commit, with a hashed file list
  (`templates/bibliography-explorer/FILES.sha256`). `researchteam update` does not sync the files. This was the
  session's recommendation, agreed with the OrthodoxLLM session; the operator has not ruled on it.
- **What was built.**
  - `scripts/bibtex_chicago.py`: BibTeX parser, LaTeX-to-Unicode decoder, name reader and a Chicago
    bibliography-entry renderer for five entry types.
  - `scripts/build_bibliography_explorer.py`: checks the input tables and writes one data file. Deterministic;
    fails closed, removing an earlier output file; standard library only.
  - `templates/bibliography-explorer/`: `CONTRACT.md`, `explorer.js`, `explorer.css`, `README.md` and the example.
  - `tests/test_bibliography_explorer.py`: 79 tests.
- **Checks.** Full suite 945 passed, 1 skipped locally (Python 3.14) before the review fixes; `tests (3.10)` and
